Privacy Policy

HoldFast: Family Stockpile (Android app)
Last updated: 1 November 2026
Developer: Nam Digital
Contact: holdfastfamilystockpile@gmail.com

The short version: HoldFast: Family Stockpile does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information. Your plans stay on your phone.

What the app does with your information

  • Everything you enter (household size, diets, quantities, shopping ticks, custom foods) is saved only on your device, in the app's own private storage.
  • The app has no internet access. It does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • The app asks for no permissions: not location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or notifications.

Files you choose to save or share

When you use Save .TXT, Spreadsheet .CSV or Backup .JSON, Android shows its own "Save as" screen and the file goes where you choose. When you use Share list, Android's share sheet opens and the text goes to the app you pick. When you use Restore backup, you choose the file from Android's file picker, and the app only receives the file you select. What other apps do with a file you send them is covered by their own privacy policies.

Android Auto Backup

Android may back up the app's saved plan to your own Google account (as it does for most apps) and restore it if you move to a new phone. This is handled by Android and your Google account settings, not by this app or its developer, and the developer cannot see it. You can switch it off in your phone's Backup settings. The in-app Backup .JSON feature is a separate backup that you control yourself.

Children

The app is intended for adults planning household supplies. It is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical or official advice

The app gives general guidance only. It is not affiliated with the UK Government or the NHS and is not medical advice. Diet and allergy tags are a guide only; always read the product label.

Deleting your data

Use More → Reset everything inside the app, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else by the developer, there is nothing for the developer to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that uses the internet), the new policy will be published at this address and the changes will be described in the app's release notes.
